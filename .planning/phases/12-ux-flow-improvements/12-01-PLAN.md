Create useUnsavedChanges hook at apps/web/src/hooks/useUnsavedChanges.ts:
- Track hasUnsavedChanges boolean state
- Provide markDirty() and markClean() methods
- Provide requestAction(fn) that either executes immediately (if clean) or stores pendingAction (if dirty)
- When pendingAction is set, consumer shows confirm dialog
- Provide confirmDiscard() (executes pending action + marks clean) and cancelAction() (clears pending)
- Export showDialog computed from pendingAction !== null
